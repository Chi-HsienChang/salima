About

I am a PhD candidate at TEILab (Taiwan Evolutionary Intelligence Laboratory), advised by Dr. Tian-Li Yu, in Electrical Engineering at National Taiwan University. Before that, I earned my M.S. in Statistics at NTU and my B.S. in Mathematical Sciences at National Chengchi University.

I currently teach programming, statistics, and data analysis as a lecturer at NTU CSIE, where I received the 2025 Outstanding Lecturer award. I'm always happy to chat about research, teaching, or building with AI. Reach me at .
